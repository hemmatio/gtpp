      GOTracker++ Guide

      GOTracker++ is a live GO Transit departure board, Union platform predictor, and commute alert tool built by Omid Hemmati.

      1. 1 Set your default trip

        Open Alerts → Commute Route, pick your home and destination stations, and tap Save Route. Tap the swap button on the Board anytime to flip directions.

      2. 2 Add to Home Screen (iPhone)

        In Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then launch the installed app icon to enable Web Push alerts.

      3. 3 Set up your alerts

        In Alerts, set your commute hours under Alert Schedule, choose your triggers, and tap Enable Web Push (then Send Test to verify).
